# How to Search for Sports Stats: The Complete Guide

Welcome to the search guide! This document shows you exactly how to find any sports statistic you need using natural language. You can search for simple stats, all-time records, recent history, or winning streaks.

## Types of Queries You Can Use

- Basic Query: Simple lookups (e.g., "Who had 10+ points?")
- Complex Query: Combining multiple conditions or filters (e.g., "Juniors with 20+ points and 10+ rebounds against Duke")
- Record Query: Finding the best or worst (e.g., "Highest points in a game" or "Who has the record for assists?")
- Last Time Was (LTW): Looking up history (e.g., "Last time a team had 50+ rebounds?")
- Streak Query: Finding consecutive performance (e.g., "3+ consecutive games with a touchdown")

## How to Search for Queries

Searching for stats is super easy! Just talk to the search bar like you're asking a friend a question. Here is how to use each type:

- Basic Queries: Basic stat search focuses on finding specific entities, like players or teams, that have reached a certain numerical threshold. Use these when you just want to know who hit a certain number.
- Example: "Players with 10+ assists"

- Complex Queries: These are for when you want to be super specific. Complex search can be performed by combining two different stats with "AND" or "OR" to see who met both requirements.
- Example: "Juniors with 20+ points and 10+ rebounds against UConn"

- Record Queries: Record Queries are designed to identify the absolute "best" or "worst" performance metrics within a specific category or timeframe. To execute these effectively, use directional qualifiers like "Highest", "Lowest", or "Most" to define your intended outcome, and always include a contextual scope—such as "in a game" or "in a season"—to ensure the returned data is relevant to the period you are analyzing.
- Example: "Lowest turnovers by the team in a season"

- Last Time When (LTW): This functions as a historical marker to tell you the most recent date a specific event occurred. It helps put modern performances into perspective by showing exactly how long it has been since a player or team achieved a similar feat. Just start by typing "last time"
- Example: "Last time my team had more than 8 turnovers in a season"

- Streak Queries: Use words like "consecutive games" or "straight games”.
- Example: "3+ consecutive games with 2+ blocks"

You can use these symbols and words in any search to get even more control:
- > (Greater Than): "Players with > 25 points"
- >= or + (Greater Than or Equal To): "Teams with >= 5 blocks" (You can also just use + like 5+)
- < (Less Than): "quarters in which team scored 2+ turnovers"
- <= or -(Less Than or Equal To): "3+ consecutive games with < 3 turnovers"
- = (Equal To): "Players with = 10 rebounds"
- AND (Both must be true): "20+ points AND 10+ rebounds"
- OR (Either can be true): "10+ assists OR 5+ steals"

How to write queries: https://search.athlytesports.com/info/query_builder_updated.html
